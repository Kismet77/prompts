Prompt 19 Improve Journal review

Your AI trading coach

Reads a month of your journal and names the pattern costing you the most, using your real numbers.

The prompt
Act as a blunt trading coach. Here’s my journal: [paste]. Find my patterns and my #1 fix for next month.

Replace [paste] with your journal export.

Before you act on the answer
Ask it not to flatter you. A coach that agrees with everything is worth nothing.
Do I understand why? · Can I verify it? · What if it’s wrong?
If you can’t answer all three, you don’t act yet.
Educational content, not financial advice. Most retail traders lose money. Practise on a demo account and only risk what you can afford to lose.
An AI is your analyst, not your decision-maker. Verify everything.
