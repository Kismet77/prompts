Before you sign up

Fair questions.

Is it actually free, or free until a paywall?

Free. Six pages, no card, no trial. The only thing it costs you is an email address, which is how the pack reaches you and how the four follow-up lessons arrive. After those, the sequence stops.

What happens to my email address?

It is stored with the email platform that sends these messages, and it is used to send you the pack, the four lessons, and occasional notes about new material. It is never sold, rented, or shared. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you immediately.

Double opt-in is switched on, so nothing is sent until you click a confirmation link. If you never click, you never hear from this address again.

Do I need a paid AI subscription?

No. Every prompt in the pack runs on the free tier of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The prompts are deliberately tool-agnostic, so they work the same wherever you paste them.

Am I too much of a beginner for this?

The pack is written for beginners, in plain English, with the blanks highlighted so you know exactly what to fill in. If you have never used an AI assistant for anything, page 2 is your starting point.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. This is educational content, not financial advice. Nothing here recommends an instrument, a trade, or a position. There are no signals, no performance figures, and no claims about what you will earn. Most retail traders lose money, and no prompt changes that. Trading carries the risk of losing your capital.
