The problem

AI can be useful. That’s not the same as saying it is right.

Ask an AI tool a question about a market and you’ll usually get a confident answer.

That is exactly why you need to be careful.

AI can miss context. It can make things up. It can reinforce your assumptions. And sometimes it can produce a very convincing answer that is simply wrong.

The answer isn’t to ignore AI.
It’s to use it differently.

The principle
The Sceptic’s Desk is built around one simple principle:
AI should be your analyst, not your decision-maker.
Research

Use it to research.

Challenge

Use it to challenge your thinking.

Find gaps

Use it to find what you might have missed.

Decide

Then make the decision yourself.

The Sceptic’s Method

A better way to use AI in your trading.

  1. 01 — Research

    Use AI to gather information, analyse markets and build context.

  2. 02 — Question

    Don’t accept the first answer. Ask what could be wrong, what is missing and what assumptions are being made.

  3. 03 — Challenge

    Make AI argue against your trade idea, not simply confirm it.

  4. 04 — Decide

    Take the information, apply your own judgement and make the trading decision yourself.

  5. 05 — Review

    Record what happened, what you got right, what you got wrong and what you can learn from it.

The point isn’t to make AI think for you.
It’s to make you think better.
