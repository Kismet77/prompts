Privacy policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data The Sceptic’s Desk collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read rather than skimmed past, which is the whole point of a policy.

1 · Who is responsible for your data

The Sceptic’s Desk is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [registered in England and Wales, company number [NUMBER], registered office [ADDRESS]]. For the purposes of UK data protection law, that entity is the data controller for the personal data described here.

You can reach us at info@scepticsdesk.com about anything in this policy.

2 · What is collected, and why

If you sign up for the Starter Pack

The name field is optional. If you leave it blank, the emails say “there” instead, and nothing else changes.

If you buy something

Purchases are handled by Gumroad, which acts as merchant of record and as its own data controller for payment data. Your card details are never seen by, or stored on, any system belonging to The Sceptic’s Desk. Gumroad passes across your email address and the details of what you bought, so that the product can be delivered and supported. Gumroad’s own privacy policy governs what it does with the rest.

If you simply read the website

The site sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. The typefaces are embedded in the stylesheet precisely so that no request goes to an external font service. Web server logs kept by the hosting provider may record standard technical information such as IP address and browser type, which is ordinary operational logging rather than anything we look at.

3 · Who your data is shared with

Not sold, not rented, not shared for anyone else’s marketing, at any price. It is processed by a small number of service providers acting on our instructions:

  • MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Lithuania) stores the mailing list and sends the emails.
  • Gumroad processes purchases and handles VAT as merchant of record.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and serve the website and its DNS.

Beyond those, data is disclosed only where the law requires it.

4 · Where your data is held

MailerLite processes data within the European Economic Area. Gumroad, GitHub, and Cloudflare are US-based and may process data outside the UK, relying on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent safeguards.

5 · How long it is kept

Your email address stays on the list until you unsubscribe, or until it stops being deliverable. If you unsubscribe, the record is retained in suppressed form so that you are not accidentally re-added, which is a requirement of the same law that gave you the right to leave. Ask for full erasure and it will be done, with the suppression caveat explained above.

Purchase records are kept for six years, which is what UK tax law requires.

6 · Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data is held about you, and get a copy.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data erased, subject to the retention points above.
  • Object to processing, or ask that it be restricted.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which for marketing means clicking unsubscribe in any email. It takes effect immediately and costs you nothing.

Email info@scepticsdesk.com to exercise any of these. There is no charge and you will get a response within one month.

If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you raised it with us first, but it is your right either way.

7 · Children

This material is intended for adults. The mailing list is not knowingly open to anyone under 18, and any address identified as belonging to a child will be removed.

8 · Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it, and anyone on the mailing list is told by email rather than left to notice.
