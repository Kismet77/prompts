AI is exceptionally good at helping us construct an argument.

That's precisely why traders need to be careful.

Give AI your trade thesis and ask:

> "Is this a good trade?"
{: .weak-prompt}

You may simply end up getting your own thinking reflected back at you.

## Instead, reverse the assignment.

Make AI attack the trade. Ask:

1. What is the strongest argument against this trade?
2. What assumptions am I making?
3. What evidence currently contradicts my thesis?
4. What am I potentially overlooking?
5. What would invalidate my thesis?
{: .attack-list}

The objective isn't to get AI to tell you whether to trade.

It's to expose the weaknesses in your reasoning before the market exposes them for you.

You can still take the trade.

But now you know what you're betting against.
